THE BOOK — 01

A nation’s history
through the camera lens.

The Eyes of Ghana explores the relationship between Ghana’s political history, the vision of Dr Kwame Nkrumah and the work of filmmaker Reverend Dr Christian Tsui Hesse, popularly known as Chris Hesse.

From the independence era to the struggle to protect historic film reels, this manuscript invites readers to look beyond familiar headlines and consider how a nation safeguards its cultural memory.
